Project Python Foundations: FoodHub Data Analysis¶

Context¶

The number of restaurants in New York is increasing day by day. Lots of students and busy professionals rely on those restaurants due to their hectic lifestyles. Online food delivery service is a great option for them. It provides them with good food from their favorite restaurants. A food aggregator company FoodHub offers access to multiple restaurants through a single smartphone app.

The app allows the restaurants to receive a direct online order from a customer. The app assigns a delivery person from the company to pick up the order after it is confirmed by the restaurant. The delivery person then uses the map to reach the restaurant and waits for the food package. Once the food package is handed over to the delivery person, he/she confirms the pick-up in the app and travels to the customer's location to deliver the food. The delivery person confirms the drop-off in the app after delivering the food package to the customer. The customer can rate the order in the app. The food aggregator earns money by collecting a fixed margin of the delivery order from the restaurants.

Objective¶

The food aggregator company has stored the data of the different orders made by the registered customers in their online portal. They want to analyze the data to get a fair idea about the demand of different restaurants which will help them in enhancing their customer experience. Suppose you are hired as a Data Scientist in this company and the Data Science team has shared some of the key questions that need to be answered. Perform the data analysis to find answers to these questions that will help the company to improve the business.

Data Description¶

The data contains the different data related to a food order. The detailed data dictionary is given below.

Data Dictionary¶

  • order_id: Unique ID of the order
  • customer_id: ID of the customer who ordered the food
  • restaurant_name: Name of the restaurant
  • cuisine_type: Cuisine ordered by the customer
  • cost_of_the_order: Cost of the order
  • day_of_the_week: Indicates whether the order is placed on a weekday or weekend (The weekday is from Monday to Friday and the weekend is Saturday and Sunday)
  • rating: Rating given by the customer out of 5
  • food_preparation_time: Time (in minutes) taken by the restaurant to prepare the food. This is calculated by taking the difference between the timestamps of the restaurant's order confirmation and the delivery person's pick-up confirmation.
  • delivery_time: Time (in minutes) taken by the delivery person to deliver the food package. This is calculated by taking the difference between the timestamps of the delivery person's pick-up confirmation and drop-off information

numerical_cols = df.select_dtypes(include=['number']).columns
categorical_cols = df.select_dtypes(include=['object']).columns

# Fill missing values for numerical columns with median
df[numerical_cols] = df[numerical_cols].fillna(df[numerical_cols].median())

# Fill missing values for categorical columns with mode
for col in categorical_cols:
    mode_value = df[col].mode()
    if not mode_value.empty:
        df[col] = df[col].fillna(mode_value[0])

Observations:¶

Mode is used for categorical columns; fills the missing values with that of the most commonly used restaurant name.¶

Conclusion and Recommendations¶

Question 17: What are your conclusions from the analysis? What recommendations would you like to share to help improve the business? (You can use cuisine type and feedback ratings to drive your business recommendations.) [6 marks]¶

Conclusions:¶

  • Most orders are $10-$20: This suggests that a large portion of customers are making moderate-sized orders, which may indicate that most customers are not opting for expensive or small, budget-friendly orders. Knowing this helps the company understand the price point that most customers find reasonable and popular. This can influence marketing strategies, promotional pricing, and packaging.
  • Moderate food preparation and delivery times: The food preparation time and delivery time for orders tend to be within a reasonable range. However, there might still be a few outliers where preparation and delivery times are excessively long. The company should monitor these timeframes closely to ensure that they do not negatively impact customer satisfaction. Customers might be unhappy if delivery takes too long, leading to negative reviews.
  • Certain restaurants perform better consistently in terms of orders and ratings: These restaurants may have a strong customer base, good food quality, or effective marketing. Recognizing top-performing restaurants helps the company identify successful restaurant practices and partnerships, allowing them to highlight these restaurants in promotions or improve collaboration with those restaurants.
  • Orders on the weekends tend to take longer to deliver: This could be due to higher order volumes or potential challenges in logistics such as traffic. This insight is important as it could indicate that more resources are needed during weekends to meet customer expectations.
  • Certain customers place more orders on the app than others do: These "frequent customers" could be loyal patrons who enjoy the convenience of food delivery. Frequent customers could be important for building long-term relationships and generating repeat business.

Recommendations:¶

  • Optimize delivery times: A significant number of orders, especially on weekends, take longer than 60 minutes, which could negatively impact customer satisfaction. The company should explore strategies to optimize the delivery process, such as adding more delivery drivers during peak hours (weekends), improving route planning using real-time traffic data, or establishing partnerships with more delivery personnel. Additionally, restaurants should be encouraged to speed up food preparation times where possible, ensuring that food is ready for pickup as quickly as possible.

  • Provide incentives and loyalty programs for high-cost orders: Customers placing higher-cost orders are potentially contributing to a large portion of revenue, but there may not be any significant incentives for them to continue ordering. The company could offer loyalty programs, discounts, or rewards to customers who frequently place higher-cost orders (for example, those above $20). This would not only encourage repeat orders but also increase customer satisfaction and loyalty. Special promotions such as discount vouchers or exclusive offers for customers who consistently place large orders could help build stronger customer relationships.

  • Highlight and partner with top performing restaurants: Some restaurants consistently outperform others in terms of orders and ratings. The company should highlight top-performing restaurants on the app or through marketing campaigns. For instance, these restaurants could be featured on the homepage or promoted with special offers to attract more customers. Incentives can also be provided to high-performing restaurants to maintain their service quality and continue attracting customers, such as offering lower commission rates or enhanced visibility.

  • Target weekend orders with special offers: Weekend orders tend to take longer for delivery, and this can be a pain point for customers. The company should offer special promotions or discounts for weekend orders to encourage more orders or reduce perceived delays. This can help manage demand and reduce delivery time pressure. Additionally, optimization of delivery routes and resource allocation (more drivers, more staff at restaurants during peak times) should be considered to improve delivery efficiency on weekends.

  • Reward loyal customers: Some customers place many orders, and they could be key drivers of revenue, but they may not always be rewarded for their loyalty. The company should develop a customer loyalty program that rewards frequent customers with points, exclusive offers, or discounts. These programs can help increase customer retention and encourage these loyal customers to continue ordering. Additionally, the top 3 customers (who place the most orders) could receive special rewards such as free delivery, discounts, or even gift vouchers.
